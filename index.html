About me

I like turning messy data into something people can trust and use. As a Data Engineer at Northbridge Insurance I built a RAG chatbot that converts plain-English questions into SQL, so non-technical teammates could query data on their own.

Before that I worked as a Data Analyst in Bengaluru, then completed postgraduate programs in data science, predictive analytics, and business intelligence. Outside work I run a weekly machine learning project that predicts Formula 1 race winners.
